OBS Setup — Output (recommended)

Output modeAdvanced
Encoderx264 (or your GPU encoder)
Rate controlCBR
Bitrate3000–4000 kbps
Keyframe interval2 s (important for HLS latency)
Preset / tuneveryfast, tune zerolatency
Resolution1280×720 or 1920×1080, 30 fps
AudioAAC, 128 kbps

The server remuxes without transcoding — whatever OBS sends is what viewers get, so keep the bitrate moderate.
